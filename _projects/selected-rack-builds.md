## The Approach

Racks from several residential projects, each built to the same standards: switches and patch panels paired so every lead is short and traceable, power kept apart from data, every device and lead labelled, and room left to service the rack later.

The banner shows a full rack, with patch panels paired with the switches above them, short colour-coded leads and power at the bottom.
