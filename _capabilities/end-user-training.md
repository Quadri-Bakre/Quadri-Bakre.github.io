## How I approach it
1. **Show the everyday things first.** Lighting scenes, music, TV and heating/cooling: what the client uses every day.
2. **Use their devices.** Training on the touch panels, keypads and apps they'll actually use.
3. **Leave them confident.** Who to call, and what to try first if something doesn't work.
4. **Give honest advice.** What will actually improve their experience, not what's newest. A great 4K screen, good sound and proper calibration usually matter more than the latest headline feature.

## Why I work this way
The best system isn't much use if the client doesn't feel comfortable using it. A good handover means fewer support calls and a client who enjoys the system rather than avoiding it. The moment I work for is the client using it for the first time: one simple control for their lighting, blinds, music and security, where the technology disappears and only the experience is left.

## Tools I use
The client's own touch panels, keypads and apps.
