## How I approach it
1. **Plan on paper first.** IP schedules and topology drawings are written before configuration starts.
2. **Keep them current.** Documents are updated as the system changes, so they match what was built.
3. **Hand over everything.** As-built commissioning records, IP schedules, topology and connectivity drawings, and configuration backups.

## Why I work this way
A system is only as supportable as its documentation. Good records mean the next engineer, or me in a year's time, can understand the system quickly, restore a failed device from a backup, and make changes safely.

## Tools I use
IP schedule and test-schedule templates, topology drawings, configuration backups, Afara reports.
