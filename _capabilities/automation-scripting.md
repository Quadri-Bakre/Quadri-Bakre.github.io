## How I approach it
1. **Automate the repetitive checks.** Logging into every device to check firmware, VLANs, PoE and errors is slow and easy to get wrong by hand.
2. **Build tools that fit the job.** Afara logs into network and AV devices over SSH and HTTP, audits them, backs up their configurations and produces commissioning reports.
3. **Keep the output useful.** Reports in PDF and Word, built from the project schedule, ready for handover.

## Why I work this way
I built Afara because manual audits and checklists took too long and depended on the engineer remembering every step. Automating them makes the checks consistent on every project and frees time for the work that needs an engineer.

## Tools I use
Python, Netmiko, Paramiko, FastAPI, Git, Docker.
