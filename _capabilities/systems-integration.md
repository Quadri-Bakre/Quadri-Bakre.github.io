## How I approach it
1. **Work with the other trades.** Interior designers, architects and other trades, so the technology serves the home's design rather than dominating it.
2. **Plan the connections.** Every system's network and control requirements are planned into the network design.
3. **Give each system its place.** Its own VLAN where it needs one, fixed addresses, and the right firewall rules.
4. **Connect through control.** Lighting, HVAC, CCTV and sources are brought into one control platform where that makes the client's life easier.
5. **Test them together.** Integration is tested as a whole, not system by system.
6. **The right drivers.** Each system talks to the others through its own drivers and protocols, so the right ones have to be chosen and tested.

## Why I work this way
Each system on its own usually works. The problems come where they meet. Planning integration from the start means every system has a clear place on the network, and the client gets one home that works together instead of several apps that don't.

## Tools I use
Crestron, Control4, Loxone, Lutron QSx, RA2 and RA3, Hikvision, Polarbear and Heatmiser HVAC controls.
