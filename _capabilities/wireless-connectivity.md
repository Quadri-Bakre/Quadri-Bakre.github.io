## How I approach it
1. **Survey the space.** Coverage is checked across every floor or deck, not just near the access points.
2. **Separate owner and guest Wi-Fi.** Each SSID is mapped to its own VLAN, with access points on trunk ports.
3. **Test roaming.** Walk the property with a device and confirm it hands over between access points without dropping.
4. **Keep the internet up.** Dual-WAN failover on Pepwave, with Starlink (land and marine) on yachts, and failover tested by pulling the primary link.

## Why I work this way
Wi-Fi is the part of the system clients notice most. On a yacht, steel and glass and moving between land and sea make it harder still. Surveying, roaming tests and real failover tests mean the client gets connectivity that works everywhere they actually go.

## Tools I use
A Wi-Fi scanner for surveys and roaming tests, the Ruckus, UniFi and Aruba controllers, Pepwave and Starlink management.

## Hardware I work with
