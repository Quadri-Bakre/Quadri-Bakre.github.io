Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: FEBRUARY 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how Quadri Bakre ("I", "me", or "my") collects, uses, and protects any personal data provided when you use this website (quadribakre.com). I am committed to ensuring that your privacy is protected and compliant with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR).

1. Information Collected

When you use the contact form on this website, I collect the following personal information:

  • First and Last Name
  • Email Address
  • The contents of your message and selected inquiry topic

2. Lawful Basis and Purpose of Processing

I collect this data solely for the purpose of responding to your direct inquiries, providing professional AV engineering consultations, and maintaining professional correspondence. The lawful basis for processing this information is your explicit consent provided at the time of form submission.

3. Data Storage and Third-Party Processors

This website utilizes Web3Forms as a third-party API routing service to securely transmit your contact form submissions directly to my professional email inbox. Web3Forms does not store your submission data on their servers; it simply routes the data.

Your data is securely stored within my encrypted email workspace (protected by Two-Factor Authentication) and is never sold, leased, or distributed to any external marketing agencies or third parties.

4. Data Retention

I retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this privacy policy, which typically aligns with the duration of our professional correspondence or active project timelines.

5. Your Rights

Under the UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data I hold about you.
  • Rectification: Request correction of any inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure: Request the deletion of your personal data from my records.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact me directly at {{ site.data.profile.email }}.

6. Changes to this Policy

I may update this policy periodically to reflect changes in regulatory requirements or operational practices. Any updates will be published on this page.
